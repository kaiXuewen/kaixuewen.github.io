01 / About

Research, from perception to decision.

My research sits at the intersection of multimodal learning and embodied intelligence. I build evaluation benchmarks and learning systems that help AI understand visual environments, seek useful evidence, and make grounded decisions.

Discover my research

02 / Current focus

Embodied intelligence that knows where it is.

Building benchmarks and methods for active visual exploration, spatial reasoning, and reliable multimodal evaluation.

01

Active perception & embodied geo-localization

Teaching multimodal agents to seek evidence, understand spatial relationships, and localize themselves through active visual exploration.

02

Human-aligned multimodal evaluation

Designing benchmarks that evaluate open-ended capabilities - from creative ideas and processes to final visual products.

03

Reliable learning in open environments

Quantifying uncertainty and detecting unseen anomalies so that intelligent systems remain dependable beyond closed-set tests.
